The old model

Traditional websites are slow to build, slow to update, and poorly aligned with AI discovery.

  • Relational database and plugin-heavy stacks add overhead for unstructured content.
  • Manual content creation slows publishing and limits page volume.
  • SEO depends on constant human effort across tags, structure, and updates.
  • Static templates rarely adapt to user behavior, proof signals, or changing content.

The KreateWebsites model

Websites become AI-readable, trust-building, conversion-ready systems.

  • Generate structured, schema-rich pages from prompts, files, and content sources.
  • Use AI to create articles, images, slides, tags, and SEO-ready site structure.
  • Refresh pages daily as new data, products, and signals arrive.
  • Let humans refine design, copy, and SEO while maintaining lineage and control.
