How DataKnobs Works

From raw data to autonomous execution—built for teams that want trustworthy AI systems, not demos.

Step 1
Connect

Connect data sources—documents, warehouses, apps, and APIs.

Step 2
Transform

AI transforms raw data into structured knowledge and reusable signals.

Step 3
Govern

Apply KONTROLS—privacy, policy, monitoring, auditability.

Step 4
Tune

Use KNOBS to influence outcomes—models, prompts, retrieval, cost vs quality.

Step 5
Operate

Deploy assistants and agents—continuously updated.

Outcome

AI analysis becomes continuous, questions become conversational, and workflows become autonomous—without losing governance or control.

Why DataKnobs Is Different

DataKnobs is purpose-built for AI data products: assistants for questions, agents for action, and KNOBS to control outcomes—under enterprise guardrails.

Compared to data platforms
Databricks / Snowflake archetype
  • Excellent foundations for storage, pipelines, analytics
  • Assistants + agents often require stitching many tools
  • Outcome control tends to become custom engineering
DataKnobs: assistants + agents as a first-class layer on top of your data product.
Compared to agent frameworks
Orchestration tooling archetype
  • Fast to prototype, governance is often bolted on
  • Hard to standardize reliability across teams
  • Optimization becomes ad-hoc and difficult to repeat
DataKnobs: KONTROLS + KNOBS make guardrails and outcome control part of the platform.
Compared to “full-stack” suites
Palantir-style archetype
  • Strong enterprise footprint, can be heavy to adopt
  • Customization cycles can be long
  • Teams want faster iteration with modern AI patterns
DataKnobs: AI-native workflows with a lighter, faster product-first platform.
