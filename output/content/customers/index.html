Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)

Dataknobs’ ideal customers are data-driven companies that want to turn large volumes of structured and unstructured data into actionable insights and data products using AI. SMB companies with limited technical resources are also ideal customers because they can leverage Dataknobs’ prebuilt AI capabilities delivered in a SaaS format.

1. The Regulated Enterprise

(Finance, Healthcare, Telecom)

Large companies that need to deploy GenAI and agentic automation in a controlled, compliant, and secure manner to unlock efficiency while meeting strict governance and audit requirements.

Primary Value Driver:

Kontrols that directly address trust, safety, and compliance needs—plus pre-built Agent AI use cases for customer care and operational analysis.

2. Data-Ambitious Companies

(Finance, Health Sector)

Organizations that see data as a strategic asset and want to rapidly build AI data products and agents across multiple use cases.

Primary Value Driver:

The Kreate module to build new data signals, web apps/dashboards, and AI assistants using AI-assisted software engineering—plus pre-built use cases like Earnings Momentum Score and Komply.

3. B2B2C

Financial Planner, Health Coach, Education

Businesses that serve end consumers through advisors or experts—such as tax advisors, dietitians, and retirement planners—and want to embed GenAI and agentic AI into their products to deliver smarter, more scalable, and more personalized consumer experiences.

Primary Value Driver:

Dataknobs’ Kreate platform (including KreateBots and KreateWebsites) to instantly build AI-powered advisors and branded digital experiences that automate routine guidance, personalize recommendations, and operate at scale without losing the human touch.
