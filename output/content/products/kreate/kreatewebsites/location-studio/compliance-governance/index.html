The challenge

What teams need to solve

Regulated and franchise organizations with local publishing and review responsibilities.

Inconsistent disclosures

Required notices and approved claims vary across pages and channels.

Uncontrolled changes

Teams cannot reliably identify who approved a local edit or when it went live.

Manual compliance reviews

Periodic spreadsheet reviews struggle to keep up with daily location changes.

Capabilities

Purpose-built for distributed businesses

Combine shared controls with location-specific data and workflows.

Policy-backed validation

Check required fields, disclosures, claims, and location-specific rules before publishing.

Approval and exception workflows

Route flagged changes to the appropriate owner, record exceptions, and require sign-off where needed.

Audit trail

Retain change history, policy results, approvals, and publish evidence.

Ownership and access controls

Assign corporate, regional, and location-level roles with scoped permissions.

Continuous monitoring

Identify outdated information, publishing drift, and remediation tasks after deployment.
