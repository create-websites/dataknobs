Packages

Starter, Growth, Enterprise, and Custom

These are packaging concepts you can use commercially even if final pricing is quote-based.

Starter

Start with one use case or one product

Best for teams that want to prove value quickly with a focused scope.

Typical entry points
  • • One product such as Kreate or Kontrols
  • • One proven use case
  • • Small pilot or departmental deployment
Good for buyers who want fast validation and a lower-friction entry point.

Growth

Typical entry points

Enterprise

Adopt the full platform

For enterprise programs that need an operating layer across multiple AI and data products.

Typical entry points
  • • Full DataKnobs platform
  • • Kreate + Kontrols + Knobs
  • • Governance, operating model, and expansion roadmap

Best Fit

Which package should a customer choose?

Starter

Best for one team, one urgent problem, or one fast pilot.

Growth

Best for teams expanding from one success into a broader solution set.

Enterprise

Best for standardizing AI and data product delivery across the organization.

Custom

Best for differentiated business requirements and strategic partnerships.
