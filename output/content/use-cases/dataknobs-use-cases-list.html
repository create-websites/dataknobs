Why this matters

Most AI products stop at answers. Ours are designed to drive outcomes.

DataKnobs products are built to connect data, reasoning, and action. That means they do not just summarize information — they help users decide what to do next.

  • Structured signals instead of raw data overload
  • Conversational access instead of static dashboards
  • Agentic workflows instead of manual follow-up

KreateBots Platform

One builder layer behind every assistant, analysis engine, and agent.

KreateBots is the platform foundation behind DataKnobs products. It makes it possible to ingest data, route workflows, structure documents, connect LLM reasoning, and publish AI experiences across web, mobile, and conversational interfaces.

  • Document ingestion from multiple sources and structured JSON extraction
  • Workflow orchestration for profile-aware LLM calls and agent actions
  • Optional retrieval-augmented generation for broad and custom knowledge scenarios
  • Consistent design and delivery across assistants, analysis systems, and agents
