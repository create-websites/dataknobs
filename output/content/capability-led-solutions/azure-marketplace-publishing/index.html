FAQ

Quick answers to common questions about Azure Marketplace publishing.

How long does publishing take?

Timelines vary by offer type and readiness. Many teams reach submission within a few weeks, followed by Microsoft review cycles and iterations.

Do you support SaaS and Managed Apps?

Yes. Offer type selection and requirements mapping are part of the strategy phase.

Does publishing guarantee co-sell?

No. Co-sell depends on eligibility and Microsoft program criteria. Marketplace publishing can be a foundational step toward ecosystem alignment.

Can you help after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing support for updates, versioning, plan additions, and listing optimization.

What do you need from us to get started?

A product overview, current architecture details, and any existing documentation. We’ll guide the rest.
