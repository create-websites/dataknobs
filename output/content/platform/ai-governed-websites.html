KreateWebsites Knobs

Control how every digital experience is generated, governed, and optimized.

Knobs make the highest-impact website variables explicit, configurable, testable, and reusable across brands, markets, and Data Products.

Template knobControls layout, section order, component selection, density, responsive behavior, and page type.
Experience design
Content knobControls tone, depth, summarization, calls to action, local relevance, and approved content sources.
Message fit
SEO / GEO knobControls titles, metadata, schema, canonical URLs, internal links, entities, and AI-readable answers.
Discoverability
Personalization knobControls location, audience, market, product, language, intent, eligibility, and recommendation behavior.
Relevance
Kontrol knobControls accessibility, privacy, disclosures, cookie notices, brand rules, link policy, and release gates.
Governance
Publishing knobControls static or dynamic rendering, deployment target, update cadence, cache behavior, and rollback.
Operations
Conversion knobControls CTA, form, scheduling, lead capture, product action, next-best step, and journey sequence.
Business action
Experiment knobControls variants, exposure, success metrics, guardrails, audience splits, and promotion decisions.
