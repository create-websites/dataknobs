How it works

Watch the journey, explain the metrics, recommend the next move.

The agent continuously analyzes how users arrive, browse, buy, and use products then turns that behavior into concrete business and financial recommendations for growth, product, and operations teams.
01

Ingest commerce signals

Bring together site traffic, product views, cart activity, checkout data, purchases, product catalog details, and post-purchase usage or activation behavior.

02

Analyze product and journey metrics

Find which products attract attention, which ones convert, where users drop off, and how different cohorts behave before and after purchase.

03

Generate business insights

Explain the why behind performance changes, identify friction points, highlight winning products, and expose usage patterns tied to revenue outcomes.

04

Recommend actions

Suggest pricing, merchandising, bundling, funnel, onboarding, and lifecycle changes that can improve conversion, retention, and customer value.

Capabilities

A business analyst agent for product, commerce, and customer journey performance.

Instead of manually stitching together dashboards, exports, and spreadsheets, teams can use one agent to understand product performance, customer behavior, financial impact, and where to focus next.
01

Product performance analysis

Understand which products drive traffic, conversion, revenue, repeat purchase, and downstream usage and which underperform despite visibility.

02

Journey and funnel analysis

Analyze movement from landing page to product detail, cart, checkout, and post-purchase activation to find the real friction points in the journey.

03

Cohort and usage intelligence

Compare how different customer groups behave after purchase, including product usage depth, repeat behavior, churn, and expansion opportunities.

04

Business and financial analysis

Go beyond clicks and sessions to measure financial outcomes like revenue contribution, margin implications, average order value, and customer lifetime value.

05

AI-generated recommendations

Suggest actionable ideas for pricing, bundles, merchandising, product messaging, onboarding, lifecycle interventions, and customer journey improvements.

06

Faster analyst workflows

Help teams answer more business questions quickly by turning raw e-commerce signals into structured findings, summaries, and next-best actions.

What teams can ask

From raw metrics to business questions.

The agent is designed to answer practical questions that growth and finance teams ask every day without forcing them to manually join multiple reports.

  • Which products attract traffic but fail to convert?
  • Where exactly is checkout friction hurting revenue?
  • Which bundles or upsell paths improve average order value?
  • Which customer segments show the strongest post-purchase usage and repeat behavior?
Who benefits

One agent, multiple business users.

The same agent can support product managers, growth teams, merchandising teams, finance analysts, and operations leaders with role-relevant views of the same commerce system.

  • Growth teams optimizing funnel conversion
  • Product teams improving product adoption and usage
  • Merchandising teams refining catalog and bundles
  • Finance teams connecting behavior to revenue outcomes
Example outputs

Structured insights teams can use immediately.

The agent does not just report what happened. It explains the business meaning, likely causes, and recommended next actions in a form teams can act on.
