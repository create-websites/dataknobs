What Changed: The 2026 Market Shift

The retail image recognition market consolidated meaningfully in 2026. Anyone evaluating this space against older comparisons should know about these changes first.

Trax and FORM merged

Trax and FORM, previously two separate competitors, merged in February 2026 into a single Tier-1 incumbent combining computer vision retail execution with field task management. As of mid-2026, the combined product roadmap and support experience are reportedly still settling : worth probing directly in a sales process.

Bigger platforms are moving in

Scandit, a mobile data-capture platform, acquired shelf-audit specialist Market Lab to combine fixed-camera shelf auditing with its existing mobile capture product. Enterprise players like VusionGroup are also active in the category, alongside specialists.

The field is wider than six vendors

Beyond the vendors profiled below, market trackers also list Focal Systems, Pensa Systems, Neurolabs, Ailet, and Quant Retail as active players : this guide focuses on the six most commonly evaluated in CPG and retailer buyer comparisons.
