Delivery surfaces

One intelligence product.
Many ways to consume it.

EKIP separates the governed intelligence asset from the presentation layer, so the same trusted core can serve executives, analysts, developers, operators, partners, and customers.

API

Data Product APIs

Expose normalized signals, confidence, evidence, and metadata through secure, versioned endpoints.

  • Customer-specific keys and quotas
  • Score, explanation, and evidence endpoints
  • Batch and real-time access
AI

Copilots & Agents

Enable users to ask questions, compare options, inspect evidence, and take governed actions.

  • RAG over curated knowledge
  • Tool access with policy controls
  • Human review for high-risk actions
UI

Dashboards & Workbenches

Convert complex signals into clear decision screens for product, compliance, finance, and operations.

  • Portfolio views and drilldowns
  • Exceptions, thresholds, and trends
  • Audit-ready explanation panels
↯

Alerts & Triggers

Push intelligence when a threshold is crossed, a pattern changes, or a risk emerges.

  • Risk, opportunity, and quality triggers
  • Email, Slack, webhook, or app delivery
  • Severity and escalation workflows
▣

Embedded Widgets

Place governed intelligence directly inside websites, portals, CRM, CMS, and internal tools.

  • Score badges and explanation cards
  • Contextual recommendations
  • Compliance guidance in the workflow
↔

Partner & Customer Feeds

Package intelligence as a recurring feed for marketplaces, enterprise customers, and data teams.

  • CSV, JSONL, Parquet, and warehouse delivery
  • Contracted datasets and SLAs
  • Versioned releases with changelogs
