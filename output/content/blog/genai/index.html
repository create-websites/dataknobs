The Ethical & Regulatory Frontier

As AI's power grows, so do concerns about its misuse. 2025 saw increased focus on creating safeguards and frameworks for responsible AI development and deployment.

  • The "Take It Down Act" makes deepfake porn a federal crime in the U.S.
  • Anthropic's Claude models champion "Constitutional AI" for safety.
  • The Pope addresses AI's challenges to human dignity.
  • OpenAI delays models for additional safety testing.
