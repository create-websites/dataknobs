A Multi-Layered Governance Framework

Kontrols provides a comprehensive set of controls to ensure trust and safety at every stage of the data product lifecycle.

Data-Centric Controls

Ensure data quality, provenance, and lineage with schema validation, anomaly detection, and data drift monitoring.

Build trust at the source with clean, reliable, and traceable data.

Infrastructure Controls

Manage compute, storage, and scaling policies for performance and cost using query throttling, autoscaling, and cost governance.

Control the foundation with efficient, resilient, and cost-effective infrastructure.

Model-Centric Controls

Govern the entire ML/AI model lifecycle, ensuring fairness and explainability with versioning, bias detection, and hyperparameter constraints.

Deploy safe and explainable models, not black boxes without accountability.

Business Controls

Encode critical business policies, KPIs, and domain-specific rules directly into the system, from compliance checks to fraud rules.

Align data products with business priorities and compliance needs.

Output Controls

Manage how outputs are delivered, presented, and consumed by end-users through response filtering, ranking controls, and content moderation.

Ensure all outputs are reliable, safe, and fit-for-purpose.

Governance Controls

Provide cross-cutting guardrails for trust, compliance, and accountability with privacy filters, encryption policies, and lineage audits.

Weave end-to-end governance into every stage of the product lifecycle.
