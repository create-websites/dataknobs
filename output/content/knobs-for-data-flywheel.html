Core concept

What is a knob?

Definition

A controllable variable, signal, configuration, or decision parameter that disproportionately influences business outcomes.

Most enterprises have thousands of variables. Very few know which ones matter, how they interact, or how to optimize them continuously. That gap is where DataKnobs fits.

KNOBS vs DATA PRODUCTS

How knobs relate to data products

Data products provide context. Knobs provide control.

Both are essential. Data products tell you what's true; knobs tell you what to change.

The DataKnobs flywheel

A much more sophisticated loop than a generic "AI platform"

Operations generate signals; signals become data products; data products surface knobs; knobs drive optimization; outcomes feed back; knobs refine.

From categories to coordinates

Knobs are coordinates in a control geometry

The five categories describe what knobs are. But once you start tuning them, knobs behave like coordinates on a curved surface some independent, some correlated, some carrying disproportionate information about behavior. That's where knob intelligence begins.

Orthogonal dimensions

Independent knobs

Some knobs move behavior along independent axes. Tuning one doesn't change the others. These are the high-leverage controls small movements produce clean, isolated effects.

Correlated dimensions

Coupled knobs

Other knobs move together. Tuning one inevitably moves another. Knob intelligence identifies these couplings so optimization doesn't waste cycles fighting itself.

High-information dimensions

Frontier knobs

A few knobs sit at the frontier of system behavior small adjustments produce disproportionate outcome changes. These are the knobs EKIP prioritizes for active tuning.

This is what we mean by control geometry the structure of the knobs themselves. It's the technical foundation that lets EKIP tune principled directions of behavior change instead of guessing one slider at a time. Read the mathematics →
