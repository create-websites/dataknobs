Capability-to-solution loop

1. CreateUse KREATE to assemble the data product, experience, assistant, or agent.
2. GovernUse KONTROLS to apply guardrails, quality checks, and compliance controls.
3. ImproveUse KNOBS and experimentation to measure what matters and tune outcomes.
4. ReuseCarry the proven patterns into the next solution instead of rebuilding the foundation.
Two delivery motions

One capability foundation, different levels of customization.

Choose the motion that fits the problem: adopt a proven solution when the workflow is standard, or compose a specialized implementation when enterprise requirements demand deeper integration and governance.

Core capability system

KREATE, KONTROLS, and KNOBS separate creation, governance, and optimization.

That separation is the point: the same solution can evolve without coupling every change to the model, prompt, interface, or governance layer.

Create

KREATE

Generate and assemble LLM-powered websites, intelligent chatbots, agents, physical designs, and data products.

Govern

KONTROLS

Apply guardrails for Generative AI, including accuracy, compliance, safety, and Fact Distance-based checks.

Improve

KNOBS

Identify measurable levers that drive outcomes, then use them for evaluation, A/B testing, and controlled optimization.
