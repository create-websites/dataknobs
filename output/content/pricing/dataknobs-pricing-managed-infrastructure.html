Financial controls

Managed infrastructure with enterprise guardrails.

Required protections

  • Prepaid monthly or quarterly usage budget
  • 50%, 80%, and 100% consumption notifications
  • Customer-selected hard stop or graceful degradation
  • Written approval before budget increases
  • Workload-level metering and cost attribution
  • Daily forecasting and anomaly alerts
  • Model routing, caching, filtering, and sampling
  • Quarterly reconciliation and capacity review

What DataKnobs should not offer

  • Unlimited LLM calls or tokens
  • Unlimited data processing or storage
  • Silent pay-as-you-go overages
  • A single fixed price without workload profiling
  • Annual reconciliation after costs have already accumulated
  • Unbounded third-party API or licensed-data usage

For workloads that are particularly volatile, either customer-owned infrastructure or a larger prepaid budget commitment is necessary.

FAQ

Common managed-infrastructure questions.

Does the displayed annual price include cloud and LLM consumption?

The price shown includes the subscription to the intelligence platform and managed environment service, while the variable provider consumption is covered by a separate prepaid usage budget.

Why not include unlimited usage?

Analyzing thousands of small records or processing billions of documents or continuous multimodal streams are just some examples of workloads that may vary greatly. Unlimited pricing could either be too costly or result in low-volume customers subsidizing high-volume ones.

What happens when the budget reaches its limit?

The customer can select from various options for their policy: hard stop, prioritizing noncritical work in the queue, reverting to lower-cost processing, decreasing sampling, or seeking approval for extra prepaid capacity.

Does DataKnobs mark up provider usage?

The preferred model uses transparent provider-consumption reporting plus a clearly stated managed-environment fee. Taxes, provider commitments, and procurement charges should be defined in the order form.

Can we move to our own infrastructure later?

Yes. Portability should be included in the architecture and agreement. Migration services may be separately scoped while the DataKnobs platform subscription continues.

When should we use customer-owned infrastructure instead?

Leverage customer-owned infrastructure for significant, unpredictable, sensitive, or heavily integrated workloads, particularly when the customer has existing enterprise cloud discounts and established platform operations.
