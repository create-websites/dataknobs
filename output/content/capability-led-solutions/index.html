Our Core Capabilities: Kreate, Kontrols, Knobs

Kreate (Generation & Assembly)

Rapidly generate complex assets from LLM-powered websites and intelligent chatbots to fully realized data products. The engine for creation.

Kontrols (Governance & Safety)

Apply essential guardrails to Generative AI. Ensure accuracy, manage compliance, and measure Fact Distance to keep AI outputs reliable and trustworthy.

Knobs (Levers & Experimentation)

Identify the key levers in your data that drive outcomes. Use this understanding for reliable A/B testing and fine-tuning the customer experience.
