The enterprise AI operating model needs knobs.

Most organizations have disconnected AI assets: prompts in one place, datasets in another, models in another, governance in spreadsheets, and business outcomes in dashboards. EKIP connects these into one lifecycle.

K

Knowledge Knobs

Control what the AI knows and which evidence it can use.

  • Curated markdown and documents
  • Ontology and identity graph
  • Source quality and freshness rules
D

Dataset Knobs

Control how training, test, and evaluation datasets are selected, enriched, and versioned.

  • Golden datasets
  • High-impact samples
  • Coverage, bias, and drift checks
P

Prompt & Policy Knobs

Control model behavior with prompts, policies, constraints, and decision boundaries.

  • Prompt registry
  • Compliance rules
  • Escalation and refusal criteria
M

Model & Agent Knobs

Control which model, tool, agent path, memory, or workflow should be used for each task.

  • Model routing
  • Tool permissions
  • Agent task decomposition
E

Evaluation Knobs

Control the scorecards that define quality, risk, usefulness, accuracy, and ROI.

  • Regression tests
  • Human review rubrics
  • Production outcome metrics
R

Release Knobs

Control rollout, approvals, experiments, customer access, and rollback conditions.

  • Canary launches
  • A/B experiments
  • Versioned intelligence products
