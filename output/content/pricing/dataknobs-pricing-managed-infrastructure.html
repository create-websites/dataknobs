Financial guardrails

Managed infrastructure should fail safely financially, too.

Recommended protections

  • Prepaid monthly or quarterly provider-consumption budget
  • Configurable threshold notifications and forecast alerts
  • Customer-selected hard stop or graceful-degradation policy
  • Explicit approval before budget increases unless pre-authorized
  • Workload-level metering and cost attribution
  • Daily or agreed-frequency cost forecasting
  • Model routing, caching, filtering, and sampling controls where applicable
  • Regular capacity and reconciliation review

Avoid ambiguous unlimited promises

  • Unlimited LLM calls, tokens, GPU, storage, or data processing
  • Silent pay-as-you-go overages
  • One fixed price without workload profiling for volatile workloads
  • Unbounded third-party API or licensed-data usage
  • Capacity increases that happen without a defined approval policy

For very large or unpredictable workloads, customer-owned infrastructure or a separately negotiated committed-capacity model may be a better fit.

FAQ

Common managed-infrastructure questions.

Does the displayed annual price include cloud and LLM consumption?

No. The displayed managed-plan price combines the DataKnobs platform subscription and fixed managed-environment fee. Variable cloud, model, storage, networking, and third-party consumption uses a separate approved usage budget.

Why not offer unlimited provider usage?

AI and data workloads can vary by orders of magnitude. Separating variable provider consumption avoids hiding risk in the subscription and lets customers choose explicit budgets, alerts, and stop or degrade policies.

What happens when the usage budget reaches its limit?

The operating policy can hard-stop selected workloads, queue or defer noncritical work, switch to lower-cost processing, reduce sampling, or request approval for additional prepaid capacity. The exact policy is agreed before production.

Does DataKnobs mark up provider usage?

The preferred model is transparent provider-consumption reporting plus a clearly stated managed-environment fee. Any taxes, provider commitments, procurement charges, or other pass-through terms should be stated in the order form.

Can we move to our own infrastructure later?

Yes. Portability can be designed into the architecture. Migration services, provider onboarding, data movement, and security integration may be separately scoped while the DataKnobs platform subscription continues.

When should we choose customer-owned infrastructure instead?

Customer-owned infrastructure is usually the better default for very large, unpredictable, deeply integrated, regulated, or contractually constrained workloads, especially when the customer already has strong cloud operations and negotiated provider economics.

Are the usage bands guaranteed prices?

No. They are planning bands only. Actual usage depends on model choice, provider pricing, region, throughput, record or document size, storage, data transfer, retention, availability, and workload architecture.
