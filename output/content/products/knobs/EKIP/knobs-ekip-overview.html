Enterprise AI needs knobs, not just models.

Most AI programs focus on model selection. Real business outcomes depend on many controllable variables: what data is used, how prompts are written, which tools an agent can call, what policies apply, how risks are scored and how feedback is captured. EKIP turns those variables into governed enterprise knobs.

1

Discover high-impact knobs

Identify the small set of data, prompt, model, retrieval, workflow and policy levers that materially change accuracy, risk, cost and business value.

2

Evaluate with evidence

Run repeatable experiments across datasets, prompts, agents and workflows so teams can compare outcomes using measurable signals.

3

Govern every change

Track versions, approvals, constraints, ownership and audit trails so enterprise AI changes can move safely from experiment to production.

4

Optimize continuously

Use feedback loops, monitoring and scorecards to keep improving AI quality as markets, customers, products and regulations change.

5

Connect to business KPIs

Map technical evaluation metrics to business outcomes such as conversion, complaint risk, analyst productivity, compliance and revenue.

6

Scale across portfolios

Manage a portfolio of AI systems across domains including finance, compliance, customer calls, e-commerce, websites and operations.

Core EKIP modules.

Use EKIP as a complete operating system for enterprise AI, or adopt individual modules around your most urgent AI control problem.

D

Dataset Intelligence

Profile, score and curate datasets for training, retrieval, evaluation and monitoring. Understand coverage, freshness, bias, duplication and business relevance.

K

Knob Registry

Create a searchable catalog of controllable variables across prompts, features, retrieval, model settings, thresholds, workflows and guardrails.

E

Experiment Engine

Run controlled tests to compare knob combinations and determine which configurations improve quality, cost, latency, risk and conversion.

G

Governance Controls

Embed ownership, approval, policy, lifecycle and audit controls directly into the AI optimization process.

P

Portfolio Dashboard

Give executives a portfolio-level view of AI systems, business impact, risk exposure, evaluation status and optimization opportunities.

A

Agent Performance Loop

Continuously monitor agents and workflows, capture feedback, detect drift and push improved configurations back into production.

How EKIP works.

A practical workflow for turning AI experimentation into governed business improvement.

Map the AI system

Capture the data, prompts, models, tools, policies, users and business outcomes involved.

Identify knobs

Find the variables that can be tuned and connect them to measurable metrics.

Run experiments

Compare configurations across representative datasets and production-like scenarios.

Govern decisions

Approve, version and document changes before they affect customers or regulated workflows.

Improve continuously

Monitor outcomes, capture feedback and repeat the cycle as business conditions evolve.
