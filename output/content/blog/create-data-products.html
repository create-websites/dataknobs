The problem

Enterprise AI forgets what the enterprise already knows.

  • Knowledge is fragmented. Policies, documents, transactions, workflows, and expert decisions live in disconnected systems.
  • Data is not productized. Every AI initiative rebuilds context, pipelines, quality checks, and business definitions from scratch.
  • Optimization is blind. Teams track thousands of metrics but rarely know which variables actually change outcomes.

The solution

DataKnobs creates the missing intelligence layer.

  • Information Memory captures reusable enterprise knowledge, behavior, decisions, and outcomes.
  • Data Products package that memory into governed, trusted, AI-consumable intelligence assets.
  • Knobs reveal the high-impact variables that tune AI behavior and business performance.

Unique Dataknobs Value Proposition

Knobs are controllable levers that influence outcomes.

Dataknobs does not only create AI outputs. It identifies the levers, data signals, configuration choices, and decision variables that have the highest impact on evaluation, training, behavior, cost, safety, and business results.

Why Knobs Matter

From black-box AI to tunable intelligence.

Knobs expose the variables that let teams steer AI systems deliberately. They help leaders ask: what should we tune, what should we monitor, and which signals actually change the outcome?

Signals → Controls → Behavior → Outcomes
1. Levers

Operational levers used to steer system behavior toward desired outcomes across quality, cost, speed, accuracy, and reliability.

2. High-Impact Signals

Data points, features, and signals with disproportionate influence on model training, evaluation, alignment, and accuracy.

3. Configuration

Configurable parameters that let systems adapt to different objectives, environments, constraints, and operating modes.

4. Optimization Controls

Controls used to balance competing objectives such as accuracy, latency, cost, safety, compliance, and reliability.

5. Decision Variables

Variables that determine how the system prioritizes tradeoffs and responds when conditions, risks, or objectives change.
