What are Knobs?

A configurable layer between complex systems and the people who use them.

Knobs turn important variables, settings and behaviors into practical controls that can be understood and adjusted by domain experts—not only engineers.

01

Identify critical data points

Analyze datasets to highlight the variables that have the greatest impact, helping teams focus attention and adjustments where they can influence outcomes most.

02

Simplify software configuration

Change system behavior through a configurable layer instead of writing new code for every adjustment, enabling faster adaptation to business needs.

03

Control AI assistant behavior

Use explicit levers to tailor responses, knowledge scope, interaction style and other behaviors so AI assistants better align with the intended use case.

04

Enable experimentation

Test changes across data, websites and AI assistants, compare outcomes, learn quickly and continuously refine the experience.

Core capabilities

One idea. Multiple control surfaces.

The Knobs model applies wherever teams need to adjust behavior, explore alternatives and improve a system over time.

Data

Data-focused optimization

Knobs distill large datasets into manageable insights by exposing the variables that most influence a result. Users can then create practical levers around those variables to tune models and system outputs.

Example: In financial forecasting, teams can identify the variables that most influence a stock-price prediction and give analysts direct control over how those inputs are prioritized.

Software

Behavior modification without constant code changes

A configurable layer helps teams adjust software behavior without turning every business change into a new engineering release.

  • Faster rollout of behavioral changes and new configurations.
  • Reduced dependency on engineering resources for routine adjustments.
  • More responsive adaptation to changing business requirements.
AI assistants

Personalization and behavioral control

AI assistants need to adapt across audiences and use cases. Knobs give businesses explicit controls over how an assistant behaves.

  • Tailor tone and response style.
  • Adjust the assistant's knowledge scope.
  • Optimize interactions for specific audiences.
  • Experiment with new conversational flows.
Experimentation

Test, learn and optimize across domains

Knobs provide a consistent experimentation model that can be applied to multiple kinds of systems.

  • Data exploration: test datasets, variables and configurations.
  • Website optimization: experiment with UI, UX, layouts and content delivery.
  • AI behavior: refine prompts, knowledge bases, response mechanisms and conversational flows.
Knobs in action

From operational systems to digital experiences.

The same control-and-experimentation approach can be applied to very different business problems.

⚙️

Predictive maintenance

In factories using IoT sensors, Knobs can expose important variables such as temperature and vibration and give engineers configurable thresholds for predictive maintenance decisions.

🌐

Dynamic websites

Marketers can test page designs, headlines, calls-to-action and content variations, helping improve engagement and conversion without requiring a developer for every experiment.

🤖

AI assistants for retail

Retailers can adjust assistant behavior to match seasonal needs, refine response styles and improve how product recommendations are delivered to different audiences.

The experimentation advantage

Make iteration a built-in capability.

Knobs are more than settings. They create a repeatable way to change a system, observe the impact and refine it. That shortens the path from an idea to measurable learning and supports continuous improvement over time.

Why Knobs matter

Move control closer to the people who understand the domain.

Knobs help organizations make complex systems more adaptable by giving experts practical ways to shape behavior without having to operate at the code level.

Democratize control

Give domain experts, marketers and business leaders direct ways to experiment and optimize rather than routing every change through engineering.

Accelerate learning

Turn changes into measurable experiments so teams can learn faster, reduce the time to value and improve systems continuously.

Stay adaptable

Use configurable behavior instead of hard-coded assumptions so systems can respond more easily as data, users and business requirements change.

Built for more than engineers

The Knobs approach is designed to make system control accessible to the people closest to the decisions and workflows.
