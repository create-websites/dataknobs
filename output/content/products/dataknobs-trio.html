The gap

AI generates outputs. Enterprises need reliable products.

  • Data is scattered across formats and systems.
  • AI outputs can be noisy, inconsistent, and ungoverned.
  • Experiments often stall before production.
The answer

A lifecycle for AI-native data products.

  • Create meaningful data assets and AI agents.
  • Embed privacy, controls, lineage, and compliance.
  • Continuously tune quality, cost, accuracy, and adoption.
