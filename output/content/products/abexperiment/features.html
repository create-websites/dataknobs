The platform

Everything behind a more reliable experiment

More than a traffic splitter. Design the experiment, deliver the right experience, understand the result and maintain control over what happens next.

⇄

Flexible traffic allocation

Set control/challenger shares such as 50/50, 70/30 or 90/10. Gradually increase the challenger without unnecessarily changing existing assignments.

◇

Sticky visitor assignment

Give returning visitors a consistent experience across ordinary page loads and visits while their first-party identifiers remain available.

▣

Pages and components

Compare headlines, calls to action, content blocks, full pages and Next.js routes. Match the delivery approach to the size of the change.

⚑

Feature-flag experiments

Put functionality behind control and challenger treatments. Evaluate flags in the application and make rollout behavior explicit.

◎

Exposure and outcome events

Distinguish the treatment selected from what a user actually saw and the outcome that followed. Keep measurement tied to the right cohort.

∑

Statistical analysis

Use foundational hypothesis tests and advanced tools for paired outcomes, small samples, effects, intervals, sample planning and multiple tests.

↗

Results and experiment health

Track conversion trends, uncertainty, sample progress and sample ratio mismatch, with checks that help flag questionable results.

⊘

Safety and governance

Use controlled access, fallback behavior, QA previews and emergency disable options to protect live customer experiences.

From question to decision

Run a structured test, not a guessing game.

A clear experimentation workflow makes setup repeatable and keeps results connected to the question the team set out to answer.

01 / DESIGN

Define the hypothesis

Choose an experience, your control and challenger, the audience and the outcome that matters.

02 / ASSIGN

Split the traffic

Set a deliberate allocation. Returning participants stay on the same treatment.

03 / EVALUATE

Measure what happened

Collect exposures and outcomes. Check sample health, effect size and uncertainty.

04 / CONTROL

Make the next move

Review the evidence, keep testing, stop a risky change or plan the rollout.

Beyond a single p-value.

The question is not only whether two variants differ. It's also how much they differ, whether the test is appropriate and whether the experiment itself can be trusted.

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you begin.

What can I test with ABExperiment?

The current product foundation supports website content and page experiments as well as feature-flag-based functional experiments. The platform's broader roadmap covers AI and data-product experimentation.

How does it keep returning visitors in the same group?

The browser runtime uses first-party visitor identifiers stored in browser storage and sticky assignment. If those identifiers are removed or the user changes devices, a new identity may be assigned unless a separate authenticated identity system is configured.

Can I choose a 90/10 rollout instead of a 50/50 split?

Yes. Custom traffic allocations are part of the core functionality. New participants can follow the new split while previously assigned participants stay on their existing treatment.

What statistical methods are available?

The platform includes baseline rate, sample planning, assumption checks, chi-square, independent and paired t-tests, Mann-Whitney and Wilcoxon, plus an Advanced Statistics suite covering Fisher, McNemar, effect size, bootstrap intervals, non-inferiority and multiple-testing correction.

Does it work for HTML and Next.js websites?

HTML snippet integration has been deployed in prior development tests. Next.js page/route proxy components have also been developed; teams should verify the deployed integration for their particular app and routing model.

Can it automatically choose and release a winner?

Results, health indicators and decision-oriented components have been developed. Automated promotion should be governed by health, predeclared success criteria, guardrails and appropriate release approval; confirm your deployment's integrated decision and rollout support before enabling it.

Are AI prompt, model and data-signal tests available today?

They are product expansion directions. The experimentation architecture is designed to accommodate them, but they should not be treated as generally released modules without a separate implementation and release validation.
