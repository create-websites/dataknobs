Unified A/B Testing Across Channels

Eliminate siloed tools. Test a website UI, a conversational AI assistant, an LLM-based feature, or a data processing pipeline under one roof, with all results in a central dashboard.

Dynamic Chatbot & AI Assistant Experiments

Go beyond static pages by testing conversational flows. Experiment with dialogue strategies, tones, and personalities to measure what truly improves user satisfaction and task completion rates.

Experimentation for LLMs and Data Models

Systematically test and compare different LLM backends (OpenAI, Gemini, etc.), prompts, or data pipeline components to find the most accurate and cost-effective configuration for your use case.

Agentic AI for Automated Experimentation

Leverage autonomous AI agents to automatically generate variant designs or conversational flows. Use LLM-based agents as virtual users to simulate interactions and get preliminary results overnight.

LLM-Powered Analysis for Faster Insights

Get instant, intelligible summaries of experiment results. Let an LLM interpret statistical patterns and write implementation-ready insights, turning raw data into actionable decisions in minutes.
