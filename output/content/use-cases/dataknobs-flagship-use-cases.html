FAQ

What buyers usually want to know.

What is DataKnobs?

DataKnobs is an AI and data-product platform organized around KREATE for creation, KONTROLS for governance, and KNOBS for measurable adjustment and continuous improvement.

What does analyze, assist, and act mean?

It describes three ways intelligence can be delivered: analysis surfaces signals, assistants support human decisions, and agents can execute approved actions within defined controls.

Can DataKnobs run in customer infrastructure?

Yes. DataKnobs supports customer-owned and DataKnobs-managed deployment models, depending on security, integration, operating, and procurement requirements.

Start with one decision worth improving.

DataKnobs can help frame the outcome, identify the context and controls, and choose the smallest production path that creates measurable value.
