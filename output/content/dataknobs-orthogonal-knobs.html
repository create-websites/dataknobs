The Concept

What makes a knob orthogonal?

Orthogonal dimensions can vary separately—like the x- and y-coordinates of a point. Changing one coordinate does not determine the other.

X

A distinct question

Each knob controls a different property: freshness, reasoning depth, confidence, governance, explainability, or action.

Y

A separate setting

A system can be high on one dimension and low on another. Neither setting can stand in for the other.

Independent control does not mean zero interaction. Adjusting freshness may affect cost, and increasing automation may require stronger governance. The dimensions remain distinct even when their outcomes interact.

The Orthogonal Knob Map

One intelligence system. Multiple independent controls.

DataKnobs replaces the idea of a single AI maturity setting with a multidimensional control plane. Each knob can be configured for the product, workflow, user, and risk context.

Business specificity

KNOB 05

KNOB 08

Governance

Why One Score Fails

Different intelligence products need different coordinates.

Two products can appear equally “advanced” while requiring radically different data, confidence, personalization, and control settings. A composite score hides those differences.
