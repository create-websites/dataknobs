Four commercial motions

Package around the customer problem—not an arbitrary maturity tier.

The older Starter/Growth taxonomy overlapped with DataKnobs' Launch/Business/Scale pricing plans. These four packages instead describe the scope and responsibility of the engagement.

Fastest entry

Proven Solution

Use when the business workflow is already clear and a known DataKnobs pattern can shorten discovery and implementation.

  • Stocks and company intelligence
  • Complaint management with AI
  • Compliance / regulatory intelligence
  • AI webmaster and content operations
Focused capability

KREATE, KONTROLS, or KNOBS

Use when the organization already has a product or platform and needs one missing capability rather than a full replacement.

  • KREATE for data products, experiences, assistants, and agents
  • KONTROLS for policy, lineage, evidence, and guardrails
  • KNOBS for evaluation, experiments, thresholds, routing, and runtime control
Shared operating layer

DataKnobs Platform

Use when several teams need common patterns for building, governing, evaluating, and continuously improving AI-enabled products.

  • Multi-team AI programs
  • Reusable context and data products
  • Consistent controls and evaluation
  • Customer-owned or managed deployment
Highest customization

Strategic Delivery

Use when the customer needs architecture, implementation, governance, integration, or fractional AI/technology leadership in addition to software.

  • Custom GenAI and agentic systems
  • Data-product architecture
  • Enterprise integration and migration
  • Portfolio operating model and leadership

How to choose

Start where uncertainty is lowest.

The best entry point depends on whether you already know the workflow, the capability gap, or the operating model you want to standardize.

01

Known workflow?

Start with a Proven Solution when the business process and desired outcome are already clear.

02

Known capability gap?

Start with KREATE, KONTROLS, or KNOBS when an existing stack needs a focused capability.

03

Multiple teams?

Use the Platform when common context, evaluation, governance, and controls need to be reusable.

04

Complex transformation?

Use Strategic Delivery when success depends on architecture, integrations, organizational alignment, or executive ownership.

05

Then expand by reuse

Move from one outcome to shared capabilities only when reuse creates measurable value.

Packaging vs pricing

Scope first. Commercial mechanics second.

A package describes what DataKnobs is responsible for. The pricing model then determines activation, platform subscription, infrastructure ownership, managed operations, and provider consumption.

Expansion model

Land with value. Expand through reuse.

The goal is not to force every customer into the full platform. The goal is to make the second useful AI capability faster, safer, and less expensive than the first.

Outcome

Prove one workflow

Define the decision, data, controls, and business acceptance criteria.

Reuse

Extract shared capabilities

Reuse context, connectors, evaluations, policies, and adjustable knobs across adjacent workflows.

Scale

Standardize only when justified

Move toward platform adoption when multiple teams benefit from the same reusable operating layer.

FAQ

Packaging questions.

Is this page the same as DataKnobs pricing?

No. Packaging defines the scope of what a customer is buying. Pricing separately defines activation, platform subscription, deployment ownership, managed operations, and variable provider consumption.

Can we start with one solution and later adopt the platform?

Yes. That is often the preferred path. Start with a measurable business outcome, then expand when the resulting data, controls, evaluations, and workflows can be reused.

Do packages require DataKnobs-managed infrastructure?

No. The same commercial package can be delivered in customer-owned infrastructure or, for qualified workloads, DataKnobs-managed infrastructure.
