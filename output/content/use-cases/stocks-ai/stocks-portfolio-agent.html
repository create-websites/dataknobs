Stocks Portfolio AI Agent • Extension of Stocks Assistant

An action-oriented portfolio agent that helps investors rebalance, manage risk, and optimize after-tax outcomes.

Stocks Portfolio AI Agent extends Stocks Assistant from market intelligence into portfolio action. It takes a user’s holdings and goals, reads company and market signals, and suggests how to rebalance positions, time entries and exits, manage stop loss decisions, harvest losses, and adjust allocations across taxable and Roth accounts.

Core Features

From stock intelligence to guided portfolio action.

Stocks Portfolio AI Agent builds on Stocks Assistant by turning company-level and market-level signals into personalized portfolio recommendations tied to investor goals, timing, and account structure.

01

Portfolio-based rebalancing

Analyze a user’s current portfolio against target goals and suggest where to trim, add, or rebalance based on changing signals and concentration risk.

02

Goal-aware allocation decisions

Recommendations adapt to the user’s time horizon, risk profile, growth objectives, and portfolio strategy.

03

Entry and exit timing guidance

Use momentum, company performance, and options market demand or supply signals to suggest when to enter, exit, or wait.

04

Stop-loss and risk control ideas

Support practical trade management decisions using bearish and bullish signals, volatility context, and portfolio exposure.

05

Tax loss harvesting recommendations

Identify opportunities to realize losses, offset gains, and reposition the portfolio with more tax-aware decision logic.

06

Account-aware optimization

Help users think through where to rebalance and how to adjust holdings across taxable accounts and Roth accounts.

How It Works

Start with the portfolio, layer in signals, recommend the next move.

The agent uses a user’s holdings and goals as the base layer, then combines that with company execution and market intent signals to generate action-oriented portfolio guidance.

1

Understand the investor and portfolio

Take in stock holdings, account types, target goals, concentration limits, time horizon, and investor priorities.

2

Read signals across companies and markets

Use earnings call intelligence, momentum score, company performance score, and options demand or supply signals to evaluate each position.

3

Recommend portfolio actions

Suggest how to rebalance, when to enter or exit, where to place stop loss logic, and how to improve after-tax portfolio outcomes.

Why it matters

Investors do not just need better stock analysis. They need help deciding what to do in the portfolio.

The agent bridges the gap between market intelligence and practical portfolio action by turning signals into decisions tied to goals, risk, and tax context.

Signal Engine

One portfolio view, multiple decision inputs.

Stocks Portfolio AI Agent combines fundamental and market signals to guide how positions should be managed in context rather than in isolation.

Company Signals

Execution quality and trend strength

Evaluate whether a stock deserves a larger, smaller, or unchanged allocation based on core business execution signals.

  • Earnings call insight and guidance changes
  • Earnings Call Momentum Score
  • Company Performance Score
  • Execution durability and deterioration signals
Market Signals

Positioning, sentiment, and timing context

Help determine whether the market is supportive, cautious, or signaling a better entry or exit window.

  • Options demand and supply analysis
  • Bullish vs bearish positioning
  • Potential entry and exit timing context
  • Stop-loss and risk-management signal inputs
Tax & Account Actions

Portfolio recommendations that do not ignore taxes and account structure.

The agent helps investors think through tax-aware portfolio moves, including when to harvest losses and how to manage reallocations across different account types.

T1

Tax loss harvesting

Identify loss positions that can be harvested to offset gains while maintaining strategic portfolio exposure.

T2

After-tax rebalancing

Suggest rebalancing approaches that reduce unnecessary tax friction instead of treating all positions as identical.

T3

Roth account adjustments

Help users think through how growth-oriented or rebalancing decisions may differ inside Roth accounts versus taxable holdings.
