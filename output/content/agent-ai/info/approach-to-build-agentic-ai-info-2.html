Prioritizing Your First Move: The Pilot Selection Matrix

Choosing initial pilot projects is a vital strategic step. Aim for 'Quick Wins' that provide clear value, spark momentum, and become compelling success stories. Use this matrix to evaluate project options.

🚀 Quick Wins (Prioritize Now)

High value, easy to execute, and highly feasible. A perfect kickoff. Drives quick results, accelerates progress, and lowers risk.

🔭 Strategic Bets

High Impact, Challenging Execution. Promising outcomes but demands substantial resources. Focus on long-term strategy over short-term trials.

🌱 Incremental Gains

Minimal Impact, High Viability, Low Complexity. Simple to execute yet not transformative. Ideal for practice or limited trials.

❌ Avoid for Now

Minimal impact, high effort, and low feasibility. Costly initiatives with limited payoff that strain resources and dampen morale.
