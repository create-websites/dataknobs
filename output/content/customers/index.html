Who we serve

One architecture, different levels of complexity.

DataKnobs can operate as an enterprise AI engineering partner, a product platform for a growing business, or a focused intelligence layer for a small team or individual user.

🏛️

Regulated enterprise

Financial services, healthcare, telecom and other organizations that need AI with strong controls, auditability and operational governance.

Best fit: Kreate + Kontrols + Knobs, deployed with enterprise data and infrastructure.

⚙️

Data-ambitious company

Organizations that want to turn proprietary data into differentiated scores, data products, assistants and workflow intelligence.

Best fit: reusable data-product and agent capabilities that accelerate multiple use cases.

🌱

SMB, professional or consumer

Small businesses and individuals that need a narrow, practical capability without building a large AI engineering organization.

Best fit: productized experiences such as websites, assistants, analytics and decision-support tools.
