KreateDatasets

The cornerstone of Data-Centric AI. An advanced engineering environment for manufacturing high-quality, AI-ready data assets, especially when real-world data is scarce, sensitive, or imbalanced.

Synthetic Data Generation: Creates artificial data to solve the "cold start problem" and enhance privacy.
Advanced Curation: Uses Active Learning and Weak Supervision to build and refine datasets efficiently.
Actionable Insights: Extracts meaningful signals from raw data to fuel AI models.

KreateWebsites

An AI-powered engine to automate the creation and management of data-driven web assets. It inverts the traditional process, generating a complete website from your raw content.

Content-First Generation: Your content from Google Drive, PDFs, or GitHub dictates the design, not a template.
AI-Powered SEO & Daily Refresh: An AI agent works continuously to dominate search rankings and keep content fresh.
CMS for GenAI: A content management system built specifically to handle AI-generated content with full lineage.

KreateBots

A comprehensive, low-code framework for building the full spectrum of conversational AI, from simple chatbots to complex, reasoning-driven AI agents.

Agnostic & Flexible: Avoid vendor lock-in with support for any LLM (OpenAI, Google) and Vector DB (Pinecone, Chroma).
Robust RAG System: Enables bots to give accurate, context-aware answers from your private, proprietary knowledge bases.
Rapid Development: Accelerate deployment with pre-built components and templates.
