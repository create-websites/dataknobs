The right workshop depends on the decision you need to make.

An executive team needs portfolio choices and operating-model clarity. A delivery team needs architecture, implementation patterns, evaluation, and production controls.

What you leave with

Artifacts your team can use after the session.

The goal is not to finish with more AI vocabulary. The goal is to reduce uncertainty and accelerate a real delivery.

1

Use-case decisions

Prioritized opportunities linked to users, measurable outcomes, data requirements, and risk.

2

Architecture blueprint

A target pattern covering context, models, RAG/tools/agents, interfaces, controls, and operations.

3

Evaluation & governance plan

Quality metrics, test datasets, human review points, release gates, security/privacy controls, and monitoring.

4

Delivery playbook

Next steps, owners, dependencies, open decisions, proof-of-value scope, and path to production.
