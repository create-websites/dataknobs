The problem

Enterprise AI forgets what the enterprise already knows.

  • Knowledge is fragmented. Policies, documents, transactions, workflows, and expert decisions live in disconnected systems.
  • Data is not productized. Every AI initiative rebuilds context, pipelines, quality checks, and business definitions from scratch.
  • Optimization is blind. Teams track thousands of metrics but rarely know which variables actually change outcomes.

The solution

DataKnobs creates the missing intelligence layer.

  • Information Memory captures reusable enterprise knowledge, behavior, decisions, and outcomes.
  • Data Products package that memory into governed, trusted, AI-consumable intelligence assets.
  • Knobs reveal the high-impact variables that tune AI behavior and business performance.

Enterprise Information Memory

AI models learn. Enterprises must remember.

Every enterprise generates millions of decisions, transactions, policy interpretations, customer interactions, operational exceptions, and outcomes. Most of that learning disappears. DataKnobs preserves it as Information Memory.

What Information Memory captures

Business knowledge, operational history, policies, decision traces, user feedback, model behavior, exception handling, and outcome data organized so AI systems can reuse it.

Knob Intelligence

Knobs are the coordinates of enterprise optimization.

Most enterprises have thousands of variables. DataKnobs identifies which ones matter, how they interact, and how they should change as business conditions shift.

From black-box AI to tunable intelligence.

Knobs expose the variables that let leaders steer AI and operational systems deliberately: what to tune, what to monitor, and which signals actually change the outcome.

Operational Levers

Fraud thresholds, escalation rules, reorder points, workflow triggers.

High-Impact Signals

Signals with disproportionate influence on risk, quality, demand, or behavior.

Configuration Knobs

Retrieval depth, model routing, temperature, policy selection, retry logic.

Optimization Controls

Accuracy vs latency, quality vs cost, safety vs autonomy, compliance vs speed.

Decision Variables

Risk tolerance, customer tier weighting, anomaly severity, compliance priority.
