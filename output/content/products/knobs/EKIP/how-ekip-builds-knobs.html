EKIP in practice

How EKIP defines and builds knobs

Most knobs are hidden buried in code, scattered across configs, or implicit in business rules no one wrote down. The Enterprise Knob Intelligence Platform turns those raw, invisible settings into a governed, instrumented, continuously-optimized set of controls.

Discover the hidden ones Define them as real variables Optimize them continuously

The starting point

Before EKIP

You don't have a set of knobs. You have thousands of implicit settings half of them undocumented, owned by different teams, set once and never revisited, with no line connecting them to the outcomes they actually move.

Building knobs isn't about adding sliders to a dashboard. It's about discovering which controls exist, formalizing them, connecting them to context and outcomes, and figuring out which ones deserve attention. EKIP runs that lifecycle end to end.

The knob lifecycle

From a raw system to a tuned knob as a loop

EKIP doesn't stop at building a knob. Each optimized knob feeds learning back into discovery and prioritization, so the set of governed controls keeps growing and sharpening.

Six stages, run as a closed loop not a one-time setup project.

Stage by stage

What EKIP actually does at each step

1
Discover

Surface the knobs you didn't know you had

EKIP maps your operational state space and scans systems, configs, business rules, and model parameters to surface candidate controls including the implicit ones buried in code or tribal knowledge.

Output → a catalog of candidate knobs
2
Define

Turn each setting into a real, governed variable

For every candidate, EKIP formalizes a definition: name, type, valid range, default, the outcome it maps to, and who owns it. An undocumented hard-coded value becomes an explicit, auditable knob.

Output → a formal knob specification
3
Instrument

Connect the knob to context and measurement

EKIP wires each knob to the data products that give it context and to the metrics that capture its effect so turning it produces an observable, attributable change rather than a guess.

Output → a knob you can actually measure
4
Prioritize

Find the knobs that actually matter

Not every knob deserves attention. Using Frontier Intelligence and Information Geometry, EKIP identifies which knobs have real leverage and which operational regions are sparse, uncertain, or high-information where tuning yields the most learning per sample.

Output → a ranked tuning agenda
5
Optimize

Tune continuously, sample-efficiently, in a closed loop

EKIP moves each high-leverage knob toward its outcome, learning from every result and re-tuning as conditions shift so values never silently drift out of alignment.

Output → knobs that stay at their best setting
6
Govern

Keep it safe, owned, and auditable

Every knob carries guardrails: safe ranges, approval policies, ownership, and an audit trail of who changed what and why so optimization never runs past the bounds the business sets.

Output → controls you can trust in production

What makes the hard steps work

How EKIP knows which knobs to build first

Discovery is easy to start and impossible to finish by brute force there are too many candidates. EKIP's prioritization and optimization are powered by two ideas that tell it where to look and where to spend its next measurement.

Frontier Intelligence

Spend effort where learning is highest

EKIP targets the sparse, uncertain, and high-information regions of your operational space the frontier where a single well-chosen adjustment teaches the most and exposes the knobs that matter.

Information Geometry

Measure the shape of the control space

Information Geometry for Enterprise AI gives EKIP the math to understand how knobs relate, how sensitive outcomes are to each one, and how to reach a better setting in as few samples as possible.

Two kinds of knobs EKIP builds

It defines operational knobs and data knobs

Operational knobs steer what the system does today. Data knobs steer what the system learns next they govern the data flywheel itself. EKIP builds both, and Data Knob Intelligence is what tells it where its own blind spots are.

Operational knobs

Control what happens now

  • Fraud thresholds, pricing sensitivity, reorder points
  • Model confidence cutoffs and escalation triggers
  • Latency-vs-cost and retry/timeout settings
  • Tuned toward immediate business outcomes

Data knobs

Control what the system learns

  • Which examples to collect, label, or prioritize
  • Where coverage is thin and blind spots are forming
  • How the data flywheel allocates scarce collection budget
  • Tuned toward learning efficiency and future capability
