Demo options

Pick the session that best fits the buyer conversation

This page supports two paths: a platform-level demo or a request for one or more specific finance AI use cases.

Option 1

DataKnobs platform demo

For visitors who want to understand how Kreate, Kontrols, and Knobs work together across data products, AI assistants, and AI agents.

  • • End-to-end product overview
  • • Architecture and workflow walkthrough
  • • Governance, controls, and deployment discussion

Option 2

Specific use-case demo

For visitors who want to see a focused walkthrough of a live or near-live finance AI workflow such as market intelligence, portfolio guidance, tax research, planning, compliance, or e-commerce analysis.

  • • Select one or more target use cases
  • • Describe business context and goals
  • • Route the request with all details in email
