Why marketplace

Treat marketplace publishing as a product launch, not a listing task.

The strongest offers align buyer procurement, technical integration, pricing, onboarding, support, and partner ecosystem goals.

1

Enterprise procurement path

Cloud Marketplace provides a standardized way for customers to discover, procure, deploy, and govern third-party solutions.

  • Integrated marketplace purchasing
  • Private offers for supported product types
  • Consolidated cloud and marketplace spend visibility
2

Cloud commitment alignment

Google states that qualifying third-party Cloud Marketplace purchases can draw down on Google Cloud commitments.

  • Use “qualifying” rather than universal claims
  • Confirm product and customer eligibility
  • Position procurement benefits accurately
3

Google Cloud ecosystem fit

Publishing can make your product easier to evaluate alongside Google Cloud infrastructure, data, AI, and security services.

  • Clear deployment and onboarding story
  • Partner-facing product packaging
  • Better enterprise technical credibility

FAQ

Questions teams should answer before starting.

Use the readiness call to validate the commercial goal, product architecture, target buyer, offer type, and launch dependencies before committing engineering effort.

How long does Google Cloud Marketplace publishing take?

Timing varies with product type, vendor onboarding, pricing review, technical integration, and review feedback. We create a readiness plan that exposes dependencies before implementation starts.

What does SaaS integration usually involve?

Current Google documentation calls for backend handling of marketplace account and entitlement events using Pub/Sub and the Partner Procurement API. Usage-based pricing can add Service Control reporting.

Can you support private offers?

We can help prepare the product and operational flow. Google supports private offers for selected product types such as SaaS, VM, and Kubernetes apps, subject to its current requirements.

Do all Marketplace purchases reduce Google Cloud commitments?

No. Google states that qualifying third-party purchases can draw down commitments. The specific customer agreement and product eligibility should be verified.

Can you help after launch?

Yes. Marketplace Ops can cover product updates, plan and pricing changes, release coordination, listing optimization, and support for buyer onboarding issues.
