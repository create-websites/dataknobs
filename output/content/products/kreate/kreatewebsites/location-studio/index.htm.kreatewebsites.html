Common questions

Designed to answer the questions enterprise teams ask.

Does Location Studio guarantee that Google indexes every page?

No. It publishes crawlable, structured pages and helps monitor discoverability signals, but crawling, indexing, rich results and ranking remain controlled by search platforms.

Can local managers change prices or hours without corporate approval?

Permissions and policy rules determine what a location can propose, what can be delegated, and what must be reviewed. Sensitive claims and mandatory disclosures can be made blocking gates.

Does it keep Google Business Profiles synchronized?

There is a connector workflow design and reference integration work for authorized profiles. Actual availability depends on customer ownership, Google API access, verification, authorization and the features enabled in a deployment.

Is the platform a guarantee of legal compliance?

No automated platform can guarantee compliance with all laws. KONTROLS can operationalize approved policies, evidence, issue triage and approvals. Applicable legal rules and validation remain the responsibility of the customer and their advisors.
