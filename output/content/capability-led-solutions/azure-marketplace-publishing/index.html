Why marketplace

Treat marketplace publishing as a product launch, not a listing task.

The strongest offers align buyer procurement, technical integration, pricing, onboarding, support, and partner ecosystem goals.

1

Enterprise procurement path

Marketplace can reduce purchasing friction by letting buyers acquire eligible offers through Microsoft’s commercial marketplace and their Azure relationship.

  • Transactable offers and plans where supported
  • Private plans/offers for eligible scenarios
  • Centralized marketplace discovery and billing
2

Azure commitment alignment

Eligible Marketplace purchases can count toward a customer’s Microsoft Azure Consumption Commitment (MACC) when Microsoft’s eligibility conditions are met.

  • Use accurate “Azure benefit eligible” positioning
  • Do not imply every offer retires MACC
  • Confirm buyer and offer eligibility before using the claim
3

Ecosystem credibility

A well-built marketplace offer gives enterprise buyers a clearer commercial, security, support, and deployment story.

  • Consistent enterprise messaging
  • Clear onboarding and support model
  • Better readiness for partner-led sales motions
Quick answer

Current Microsoft publisher documentation supports SaaS, Azure Application (including managed applications), Azure Container, Azure VM, professional services, and other offer families. Exact listing and transaction options vary by offer type. DataKnobs helps you choose the right path and translate provider requirements into an executable launch plan.

FAQ

Questions teams should answer before starting.

Use the readiness call to validate the commercial goal, product architecture, target buyer, offer type, and launch dependencies before committing engineering effort.

How long does Azure Marketplace publishing take?

It depends on offer type, technical readiness, legal/commercial setup, and review findings. We first identify the critical path, then separate work your team must complete from work DataKnobs can drive.

Do you support SaaS and Azure Managed Applications?

Yes. The engagement starts with offer-type selection and requirements mapping. SaaS and Azure Application/Managed App paths have different technical and commercial integration work.

Will listing on Marketplace make us co-sell ready?

No. Marketplace publishing and co-sell are related but distinct. We can make the marketplace offer and supporting assets stronger; Microsoft determines program eligibility.

Can Marketplace purchases count toward a customer MACC?

Some can. Microsoft states that eligible, Azure-benefit Marketplace purchases can contribute to MACC when its conditions are met. We recommend verifying eligibility for the specific offer and buyer.

Can you help after launch?

Yes. Marketplace Ops can cover plan changes, listing updates, technical versioning, buyer feedback, and commercial optimization.
