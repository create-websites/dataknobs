The Problem

Customer risk is fragmented across systems.

Enterprises already collect customer-facing data across websites, location-page systems, chatbots, call centers, CRM, ticketing, surveys, reviews, social listening platforms, and data warehouses. The problem is not lack of data. The problem is fragmented intelligence.

The DataKnobs Approach

Your systems collect the signals. DataKnobs finds the risk.

DataKnobs does not need to replace existing social, contact-center, chatbot, CRM, CMS, or review platforms. It works as the intelligence layer that turns customer-facing data into complaint, compliance, and regulatory risk insights.

Three Customer Risk Surfaces

Monitor what you say, what customers experience, and what customers say publicly.

Customer Compliance Intelligence organizes enterprise risk across three surfaces so teams can connect isolated signals into a full customer-harm and regulatory-risk picture.

01

Company-Controlled Content

What your company publishes, approves, controls, or distributes.

  • Corporate websites, product pages, pricing pages, landing pages, FAQs, policy pages, and help-center articles
  • Branch, store, clinic, franchise, dealer, advisor, and agent pages
  • Approved scripts, marketing copy, email templates, and chatbot knowledge-base content
02

Customer Interactions

What happens when customers interact directly with your company.

  • Chatbot transcripts, AI assistant conversations, live chat, and agent chat
  • Call-center transcripts, speech-to-text outputs, escalation notes, and agent dispositions
  • Support tickets, CRM cases, emails, surveys, complaint forms, and case records
03

External Customer Signals

What customers say outside your company’s direct control.

  • Google reviews, app-store reviews, Trustpilot, BBB, and marketplace reviews
  • Social media posts, Reddit, public forums, community groups, and YouTube comments
  • Public complaint databases, consumer feedback, and reputation signals

Use Case Modules

Start with one channel. Expand into omnichannel customer-risk intelligence.

Customer Compliance Intelligence can be deployed as a focused scan or as a cross-channel intelligence layer.

Website Compliance Monitoring

Scan product pages, pricing pages, policy pages, campaigns, and help content for missing disclosures, misleading claims, broken links, outdated language, and required-content gaps.

Multi-Location Page Compliance

Monitor branch, store, clinic, franchise, dealer, advisor, and agent pages for local-page deviations, inconsistent offers, missing disclosures, incorrect hours, unsupported claims, and licensing gaps.

Chatbot Compliance Monitoring

Review AI assistant and chatbot transcripts for complaints, incorrect answers, hallucinations, missed escalations, sensitive-topic issues, and recurring customer confusion.

Call-Center Transcript Compliance

Analyze call transcripts for complaint detection, complaint-handling quality, agent script deviations, missed escalations, regulatory risk, and recurring issue themes.

Review & Social Complaint Intelligence

Analyze Google reviews, app reviews, BBB complaints, social posts, forums, and public feedback to detect early warning signals, reputational risk, and external customer-harm patterns.

Evidence-First Intelligence

Every finding should explain what happened, where it happened, why it matters, and what to fix first.

DataKnobs produces structured outputs that compliance, legal, risk, operations, customer experience, product, and executive teams can act on.

For every issue, DataKnobs can provide

  • Source channel, URL, transcript, review, ticket, record ID, product, team, or location
  • Exact evidence, quote, page text, or conversation segment
  • Issue type, complaint theme, risk theme, severity score, and confidence score
  • Policy, control, or regulatory mapping based on your taxonomy
  • Recommended next action, owner assignment, review status, and remediation workflow

Daily and weekly intelligence reports

  • Daily risk brief for high-risk complaints, chatbot failures, location-page issues, and urgent remediation
  • Weekly complaint intelligence report for themes, trends, affected products, locations, and teams
  • Executive dashboard for severity, volume, top channels, top risk themes, and remediation status
