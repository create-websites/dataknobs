From spreadsheet maintenance to agentic legal operations

The old process was manual. The new process is continuous.

Previously, legal teams maintained spreadsheets of applicable laws, revisited government sites from time to time, interpreted changes manually, and then updated product and process requirements. The Applicable Laws AI Agent automates that monitoring and first-pass extraction while keeping legal teams in control of final review.
Before

Manual legal tracking workflow

Applicable law management often depended on spreadsheet maintenance and periodic review cycles that made it difficult to catch new laws and changes early.

  • Legal maintains Excel sheets of applicable laws
  • Teams manually revisit government websites
  • New changes may be detected late
  • Product waits for legal interpretation before action
  • Knowledge is fragmented across sheets, notes, and emails
After

AI-powered legal change workflow

The agent watches official sources continuously, extracts relevant obligations, and alerts legal and product teams with structured, reviewable changes.

  • Monitor official legal and regulatory websites at regular intervals
  • Detect new laws, amendments, and requirement changes
  • Extract obligations into structured compliance requirements
  • Notify legal and product teams about what changed and why it matters
  • Keep a continuously refreshed legal intelligence layer instead of static sheets
How it works

Track, detect, extract, notify.

The Applicable Laws AI Agent turns official legal updates into enterprise-ready obligations that legal teams can review and product teams can implement.
01

Track applicable laws

Identify privacy and financial laws that matter to the enterprise based on product model, data practices, and jurisdictions.

02

Detect changes

Monitor government and regulatory websites on a regular interval for new laws, updates, guidance changes, and revised obligations.

03

Extract requirements

Convert legal text into structured obligations such as consent, access, deletion, correction, notice, minimization, and opt-out requirements.

04

Notify and coordinate

Alert legal and product teams, update the applicable-law record, and help teams review which product workflows or policies must change.

Who benefits

Built for collaboration between legal and product teams.

The agent helps legal teams review changes faster while giving product and operations teams the clarity they need to update workflows, customer experiences, notices, and control points.

  • Legal teams reviewing new laws and amendments
  • Privacy and compliance teams maintaining requirement libraries
  • Product teams aligning journeys with current obligations
  • Operations teams updating DSAR and consent-handling workflows
Why it matters

Products cannot comply with laws that arrive too late.

When legal tracking is manual, updates may be missed or delayed. The result is slower review cycles, fragmented knowledge, and product gaps. The agent reduces that delay and turns legal change into a continuous operating process.

  • Earlier visibility into new requirements
  • Less repetitive monitoring work for legal teams
  • Faster handoff from legal change to product change
  • Better confidence that products and processes remain aligned
