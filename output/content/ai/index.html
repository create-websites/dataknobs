A different approach

Do not force every business problem through the same AI model

DataKnobs uses a multi-paradigm AI stack. The goal is not to maximize model novelty; it is to choose the simplest, strongest technique that converts available data into a measurable business outcome.

1

Foundation

Bring together raw structured and unstructured operational data with the context needed to interpret it.

2

Transformation

Engineer signals and apply predictive, statistical, generative or agentic intelligence according to the use case.

3

Activation

Deliver decision-ready intelligence through applications, workflows, APIs, agents and user-facing data products.

Spectrum of AI capabilities

Use the right intelligence for the job

Predictive ML, language models, statistical techniques and autonomous agents solve different classes of problems. DataKnobs brings them together around the data and the desired outcome.

Predictive AI

Predictive intelligence

Custom machine-learning models extract forward-looking signals from operational data so teams can act before an outcome occurs.

Real-world caseData-center switchgear health

Voltage and telemetry data are converted into equipment health scores to support predictive maintenance and risk reduction.

Generative AI

Large language models

LLMs transform unstructured content into structured insight, summaries, explanations, recommendations and interactive experiences.

Real-world caseStock earnings-call analysis

Transcripts are analyzed for sentiment, risks and opportunities, then converted into structured summaries for faster investment research.

Statistical AI

Operational intelligence

Classical methods remain ideal for frequent, explainable decisions where computational efficiency and statistical rigor matter.

Typical capabilitiesExperimentation, anomaly detection & trends

A/B testing, statistical comparisons, anomaly detection and trend analysis provide measurable operating feedback.

Agentic AI

Autonomous AI systems

Agents orchestrate tools, data, models and workflows to complete multi-step business tasks with bounded autonomy.

Major advancementFrom answers to actions

Agentic systems can monitor conditions, analyze context, prepare decisions and trigger workflows instead of only generating text.

Design principle

The model is one component. The system is the product.

Production value comes from connecting enterprise context, intelligence, controls and user workflows—not from choosing a model in isolation.

Context first

Give intelligence the business data, definitions and history required to make the output meaningful.

Measure continuously

Track quality, cost, latency and business outcomes so model and workflow choices remain evidence-based.

Activate safely

Move from insight to automation with explicit controls, review points and operating boundaries appropriate to the use case.

AI agents for business operations

Integrated systems that analyze, decide and prepare action

Analytics

E-commerce Analytics Agent

Compliance

Compliance Analysis Agent

Reviews chats, emails and calls for quality issues, complaint signals and compliance concerns.

Impact: surfaces recurring patterns and reduces the amount of interaction review that must be performed manually.
