Common engine

Every use case follows the same productization path.

EKIP makes portfolio expansion practical because the core pattern repeats: raw data becomes governed signals, signals become data products, and data products become APIs, apps, dashboards, agents, and workflows.

01 Raw data

Collect

Transcripts, market data, court records, web pages, forms, sensor logs, documents, and customer interactions.

02 Signals

Transform

Scores, themes, labels, embeddings, extracted facts, anomalies, events, evidence, and confidence bands.

03 Controls

Govern

Validity, quality, policy, risk, lineage, approvals, release gates, and monitoring checks.

04 Product

Package

Schemas, documentation, versioning, dashboards, APIs, alert definitions, and customer-specific access.

05 Decisions

Operate

Agents, assistants, workflows, enterprise dashboards, alerts, reviews, and closed-loop improvements.

Reusable components

The portfolio compounds over time.

Each new EKIP product reuses prior capabilities: ingestion patterns, signal definitions, quality checks, governance contracts, reviewer workflows, APIs, access control, dashboards, and evidence trails.

  • Faster launch of new intelligence products
  • Consistent governance across domains
  • Shared signal registry and evaluation assets
  • Reusable customer delivery channels

Product map

How the product families connect to KREATE, KONTROLS, and KNOBS.

KREATE

Build and deliver intelligence. KreateDatasets, KreateWebsites, KreateBots, and KreateDataProduct APIs turn portfolio assets into apps, dashboards, copilots, web products, and feeds.

KONTROLS

Govern each product family. Compliance, risk, privacy, quality, release, and operational checks become executable controls attached to data products.

KNOBS

Optimize outcomes. Tune prompts, models, thresholds, rules, datasets, experiments, features, and workflow routing for measurable improvement.

EKIP

Unify the portfolio. EKIP provides the enterprise knowledge, intelligence, and productization layer that makes each use case repeatable.
