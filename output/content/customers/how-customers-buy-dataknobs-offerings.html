Engagement options

Choose the smallest DataKnobs engagement that can create real value.

Start with a proven solution, a focused capability, the full platform, or expert-led delivery. The right choice depends on how clearly the problem is defined, how much customization is required, and whether the organization needs one workflow or a repeatable AI operating model.

Four engagement paths

Different entry points. A common architecture underneath.

Customers do not need to buy the entire platform on day one. The engagement can begin where the business value is clearest and expand as reusable capability is proven.

Solution

Proven use case

Adapt a workflow DataKnobs has already built—such as complaint intelligence, tax research, stock analysis, compliance or AI-powered web operations.

  • Fastest when the problem is already clear
  • Reuses tested solution patterns
  • Can be customized to customer data and workflow
Capability

Focused product

Adopt the specific capability that closes the immediate gap, then add adjacent capabilities later.

  • KREATE for creation and productization
  • KONTROLS for governance and assurance
  • KNOBS for tuning and experimentation
Platform

DataKnobs platform

Create a repeatable operating layer for building, governing and improving multiple AI/data products across teams.

  • Shared architecture and controls
  • Portfolio-level standards
  • Reusable data, evaluation and agent patterns
Expert team

Custom delivery

Bring in DataKnobs for architecture, GenAI/agent design, data-product engineering and integration when the environment is unique.

  • Complex enterprise integration
  • Security and governance constraints
  • New capabilities or unusual workflows

Expansion model

Land on value. Expand on reuse.

The preferred expansion path is not “sell more software.” It is to identify which parts of a successful workflow should become reusable enterprise capability.

A

Solution → capability

A successful use case reveals reusable data, evaluation, controls, prompts, integrations and user-experience patterns.

B

Capability → platform

When several teams need the same capabilities, standardize them through shared platform services and governance.

C

Platform → portfolio

Use common controls and KNOBS to compare, operate and improve many AI products without losing local flexibility.

Starting point by customer type

Match the engagement to organizational maturity.

Enterprise transformation team

Usually a platform or expert-delivery starting point because standards, governance and cross-team reuse matter.

Functional team with one urgent need

Usually a proven solution or focused capability that can demonstrate value without a broad platform program.

Data-ambitious growth company

Often capability-first: turn proprietary data into a differentiated product, assistant or workflow, then reuse the pattern.

SMB or professional workflow

Usually productized and outcome-specific: websites, assistants, analytics or narrow automation with minimal infrastructure overhead.

FAQ

Choosing an engagement path.

What is the fastest way to start?

If the workflow is already clear, a proven solution or focused capability is usually the fastest starting point. Broader platform adoption fits multi-team or portfolio needs.

Can an engagement expand later?

Yes. A customer can begin with one solution or capability and expand into additional products, governance controls or platform-wide standards as value is proven.

When should we use the expert team?

Expert-led delivery fits organizations with unique data, integration, security, architecture or operating-model requirements that cannot be addressed by a standard product configuration alone.

Bring the problem. We’ll help choose the entry point.

You do not need to decide “platform vs. product vs. consulting” before the first conversation. Start with the workflow, users, data, constraints and desired outcome.
