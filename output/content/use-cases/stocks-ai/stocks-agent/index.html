Options + market intelligence

Once you like the company, decide when and how to act.

Stocks Agent analyzes options-market positioning to identify demand, resistance, and normalized distance from those levels.

  • Demand intelligence: identify potential price areas where options activity suggests stronger demand or market interest.
  • Resistance intelligence: surface areas where options positioning may influence the stock or create meaningful resistance.
  • Normalized distance: compare how close different stocks are to demand or resistance even when their prices differ substantially.
  • Decision context: combine company quality with trading setup instead of viewing technical or options signals in isolation.

Strategy intelligence

Move from “what is happening?” to “what can I evaluate?”

Stocks Agent includes a growing options-strategy library. The objective is not to recommend complexity; it is to connect market view, objective, portfolio, and risk to an appropriate strategy to evaluate.

Income

Covered Call

Evaluate premium income against assignment risk and upside surrendered on shares you already own.

Entry

Cash-Secured Put

Evaluate getting paid while waiting to buy a stock at a strike price you are willing to accept.

Protection

Collar

Explore downside protection while potentially offsetting part of the hedge cost by selling a call.

Directional

Long Call / Long Put

Model defined-cost directional exposure without taking the same exposure as buying or shorting shares.

Defined Risk

Bull / Bear Spreads

Compare directional views with capped risk and defined payoff structures.

Volatility

Straddle & More

Evaluate scenarios where the magnitude of movement may matter more than direction.
