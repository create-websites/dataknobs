From complaint transcripts to governed agentic action

From transcripts to decisions

Complaint management is a data-product + agent problem

Calls, chats and messages contain signals about customer harm, operational failure, emerging risk and regulatory exposure. The challenge is turning unstructured interactions into consistent decisions and actions.

🎧

Unstructured evidence

Complaint intent is often buried in long conversations, indirect language and product-specific context.

⚖️

Risk-sensitive decisions

Different products, regulations and customer circumstances require different thresholds, escalation rules and disclosures.

📈

Volume + drift

Behavior changes over time: customer language, products, agents, policies and model performance all drift.

Reference architecture

Build intelligence first, then add bounded agency

1

Ingest

Calls, transcripts, chats, metadata and product context.

2

Structure

Entities, summaries, complaint intent, reason codes and evidence spans.

3

Score

Complaint likelihood, severity, risk, confidence and escalation priority.

4

Decide

Apply thresholds, product rules, policy and human-review criteria.

5

Act

Create case, route, request information, draft response or escalate.

Agentic layer

What the agent should: and should not: do

Good agent responsibilities

Gather missing case context from approved systems.
Explain why the case crossed a threshold and surface supporting transcript evidence.
Draft a resolution path or next-best action under product policy.
Open or update cases and route low-risk tasks within permission scope.
Escalate uncertainty, high severity or policy conflicts to a human.

Keep bounded or human-approved

Material customer compensation beyond predefined limits.
Final legal or regulatory conclusions without review.
Changes to source-of-truth records without validation and authorization.
Actions where evidence is incomplete, contradictory or low confidence.
Broad access to unrelated customer or product data.
KNOBS for complaint AI

Operate the system with thresholds, experiments and feedback

A complaint system should not have one static “AI accuracy” number. Tune the operating points by business risk and continuously test whether the system improves outcomes.

Classification knob

Thresholds for complaint/non-complaint, category, severity and confidence.

Escalation knob

When to send a case to a specialist, supervisor or compliance team.

Automation knob

Which actions are recommend-only, approval-gated or fully automated.

Evaluation knob

Compare prompts, models, rules and workflows against labeled outcomes and operational metrics.
