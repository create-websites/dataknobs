Customer outcomes · Enterprise to SMB

Useful AI starts with a real business problem.

DataKnobs helps organizations turn proprietary data, documents and operating context into governed intelligence: data products, assistants, agents, websites and decision-support workflows. The goal is not to add AI everywhere. It is to create reusable capabilities that make the next valuable use case faster to deliver.

How value is created

Build once. Learn. Reuse. Improve.

The strongest customer engagements produce both an outcome now and reusable intelligence for later. DataKnobs combines KREATE, KONTROLS and KNOBS so data, governance and experimentation are designed together rather than bolted on after a prototype.

1

Create signals

Turn raw data into decision-ready scores, classifications, features and health indicators.

2

3

Expose intelligence through products, portals, assistants, APIs and workflow interfaces.

4

Activate agents

Move from insight to governed action with tools, approvals, policies and measurable autonomy.

Who we serve

The architecture scales with the problem.

The same principles can support a regulated enterprise program, a data-ambitious growth company or a narrow productized workflow for a small team.

Enterprise

Regulated and complex organizations

Best when AI must integrate with enterprise data and infrastructure while meeting security, audit and governance requirements.

Growth

Data-ambitious companies

Best when proprietary data can become differentiated scores, assistants, data products or workflow intelligence across several use cases.

Focused

SMBs, professionals and individual users

Best when a narrow business outcome can be delivered through a productized website, assistant, analytics workflow or decision-support experience.
