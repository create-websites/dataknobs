DataKnobs selection framework

Evaluate the data as a product, not just a feed.

The right provider depends on the exact outcome, evaluation method, governance requirement and operational constraint.

Research

Start with representative evaluation questions

Define instruments, date ranges, corporate actions, quote conditions, Greeks, surfaces and required reproducibility before testing vendors.

Kontrols

Validate completeness and consistency

Measure missing observations, stale quotes, crossed markets, timestamp quality, symbol mapping, split adjustments and calculation differences.

Knobs

Optimize quality, cost and latency together

Provider, granularity, refresh frequency, history depth, field selection and derived analytics are knobs that directly affect model and product outcomes.
