Closing the Gap Between R&D and Validation

“Data products generally require validation both of whether the algorithm works, and of whether users like it... builders face an inherent tension between how much to invest in the R&D upfront and how quickly to get the application out to validate that it solves a core need.”
– Harvard Business Review

This core challenge leads to failure. Products are either over-engineered without user feedback or shipped too quickly without the necessary quality and governance. Dataknobs provides the integrated factory floor to solve this.

An Integrated Data Product Factory

From raw data to governed applications, our platform streamlines the entire lifecycle.

Kreate

The creation engine. Transform raw data into interactive, intelligent products. Kreate unifies GenAI, Agentic AI, and traditional engineering to generate new data signals, interfaces, and assistants with speed and reliability.

Knobs

The experimentation and diagnostics layer. Knobs are tunable levers that allow you to experiment, validate, and optimize data products, from algorithms to user interfaces. They bridge the gap between R&D and user validation.

Validate Performance & Adoption

Kontrols

The governance and trust engine. Kontrols embeds guardrails across data, infrastructure, models, and business processes, ensuring every data product remains secure, compliant, explainable, and aligned with business goals.
