Four adoption questions

What outcome should change?Choose a measurable workflow outcome: not “deploy an agent.”
What is the right human-agent mode?Author, edit, direct or orchestrate depending on consequence, ambiguity and reversibility.
What authority is justified?Start with the smallest permission set, bounded destinations and explicit approval for consequential operations.
What evidence proves it works?Evaluate task success, safety, cost, latency, exception rate and downstream business impact.

A better replacement for the old maturity chart

Rather than implying a universal numeric maturity score, use observable capabilities and controls.

1

Explore

Prompting, sandbox prototypes, no real-world authority.

2

Assist

Retrieval and recommendations with human action.

3

Delegate

Bounded actions under policy and approval.

4

Orchestrate

Multi-step or multi-agent workflows with explicit handoffs.

5

Optimize

Controlled experimentation and evaluation improve operating parameters over time.
