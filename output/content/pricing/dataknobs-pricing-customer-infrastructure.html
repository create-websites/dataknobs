Pricing units

Know what a Data Product and a governed Knob mean commercially.

DP

Production Data Product

A governed intelligence asset with a defined business purpose, owners, source boundaries, output contract, quality controls, and lifecycle.

K

Governed Knob

An explicit adjustable control—such as data selection, model choice, threshold, prompt, tool access, autonomy, policy, or evaluation setting—that can be measured and governed.

E

Environment

A distinct production or non-production runtime boundary with its own configuration, access, release, and operational controls.

S

Support & services

Architecture, custom connectors, regulated controls, migration, SLAs, and delivery services can add scope beyond the standard platform plan.

FAQ

Common customer-owned pricing questions.

Why pay a platform fee if we provide the infrastructure?

Infrastructure runs the workload. The DataKnobs subscription covers governed Data Products, Knobs, lineage, evaluation, monitoring, experimentation, optimization, and the management layer that makes intelligence reusable across applications and workflows.

Who pays for cloud, warehouse, and LLM usage?

The customer contracts with and pays its selected cloud, data-platform, model, GPU, and third-party providers directly.

Does DataKnobs charge per user or per agent?

These plans are primarily priced around production Data Products and governed intelligence capacity rather than seats. Final order terms can include additional limits or service dimensions where required.

What counts as one production Data Product?

A production Data Product is a governed intelligence asset with a defined business purpose, owners, source boundaries, output contract, quality controls, and lifecycle. Materially different output contracts or unrelated domains may be scoped as separate Data Products.

Why is activation separate from the annual subscription?

Activation covers one-time work such as discovery, integration, configuration, evaluation, security alignment, testing, and launch. Separating it keeps implementation effort distinct from recurring platform value.

Can DataKnobs provide managed infrastructure later?

Yes. A customer-owned deployment can be complemented or replaced by a separately scoped DataKnobs-managed environment when the workload and commercial requirements make that useful.

Are the published prices final?

They are planning prices and ranges. Final terms depend on production scope, integrations, environments, security, compliance, support, and the definition of governed Data Products and Knobs.
