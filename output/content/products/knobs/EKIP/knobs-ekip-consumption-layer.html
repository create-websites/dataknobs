EKIP turns intelligence products into channels people actually use.

A knowledge platform is valuable only when the governed intelligence reaches the moment of decision. The EKIP Consumption Layer packages datasets, scores, controls, ontologies, and explanations into apps, APIs, copilots, dashboards, alerts, and enterprise workflows.

One intelligence product. Many delivery surfaces.

EKIP separates the intelligence asset from the user interface. The same governed source can power a dashboard for executives, an API for developers, an agent for analysts, and an alert stream for operations.

API

Data Product APIs

Expose normalized signals, confidence, evidence, and metadata through versioned APIs.

  • Customer-specific keys and quotas
  • Score, explanation, and evidence endpoints
  • Batch and real-time access patterns
AI

Copilots & Agents

Let business users ask questions, compare options, inspect evidence, and take governed actions.

  • RAG over curated knowledge
  • Tool access with policy controls
  • Human review for risky actions
UI

Dashboards & Workbenches

Turn complex signals into clear decision screens for product, compliance, finance, and operations teams.

  • Portfolio views and drilldowns
  • Exceptions, thresholds, and trends
  • Audit-ready explanation panels

Alerts & Triggers

Push changes when the intelligence crosses a threshold or a risk pattern emerges.

  • Risk, opportunity, and quality triggers
  • Email, Slack, webhook, or app notifications
  • Severity and escalation workflow

Place governed intelligence directly inside websites, portals, CRM, CMS, or internal tools.

Partner & Customer Feeds

What makes this different?

Most AI projects stop at a model demo. EKIP continues to the channel, the workflow, the audit trail, and the measurable business outcome.

Delivery knobs controlled by EKIP

  • Audience knob: executive, analyst, developer, customer, regulator.
  • Freshness knob: real-time, daily, weekly, or release-based.
  • Evidence knob: summary only, source trace, full lineage, or audit bundle.
  • Actionability knob: insight, recommendation, workflow task, or automated trigger.
  • Monetization knob: internal tool, paid API, partner feed, or managed service.
