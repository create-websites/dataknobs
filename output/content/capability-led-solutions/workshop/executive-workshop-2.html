About Prashant Dhingra

Prashant Dhingra is an experienced technology leader specializing in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Generative AI (GenAI), and emerging Agentic AI systems that power intelligent data-driven products.

Prashant has hands-on experience designing and building AI systems that integrate Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), vector databases, and enterprise data platforms. His work emphasizes practical, production-grade AI solutions that combine structured enterprise data, document processing, and modern AI models to deliver measurable business outcomes.

"Built end-to-end AI systems combining document ingestion, OCR pipelines, structured data extraction, vector search, and LLM reasoning to support complex enterprise use cases such as financial analysis, compliance workflows, and intelligent research assistants."
