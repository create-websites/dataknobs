FAQ

Product questions.

Are DataKnobs products separate from the platform?

Products are packaged experiences and capabilities built on the same KREATE, KONTROLS, and KNOBS foundation. Customers can adopt a focused product first and expand later.

Does every product include an AI agent?

No. Some products primarily analyze or assist. Agentic action is introduced only where the workflow, controls, approvals, and risk model support it.

Can products use customer-owned models and infrastructure?

Yes. Deployment can use customer-owned cloud, data platforms, model providers, and security boundaries, or a separately scoped DataKnobs-managed environment.

Pick the smallest product that can prove business value.

DataKnobs can map a workflow to the right product, deployment model, controls, and evaluation plan without requiring a platform-wide commitment on day one.
