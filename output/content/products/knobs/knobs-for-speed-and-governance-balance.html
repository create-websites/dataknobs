Three Knob Types

Each knob serves both masters

A Knob is any controllable variable that disproportionately influences model behavior or business outcomes. EKIP structures them into three types each one accelerates creation while embedding governance by design.

Selection Knobs

Which examples matter most

Identifies the high-information examples that drive outcomes

Speed Focuses training on sparse, high-signal data fewer examples, faster iteration cycles
Govern Coverage maps prove completeness; auditors see exactly which regions were sampled and why
Speed Eliminates redundant annotation work teams stop labeling noise
Govern Identifies under-represented edge cases before they cause production failures
Creation Knobs

Synthetic data, on demand

Generates data that fills gaps without drifting outside principled regions

Speed Accelerates the data flywheel no waiting for rare real-world events to appear
Govern Information geometry bounds constrain generation to principled, in-distribution regions
Speed Enables stress-testing without costly real-world data collection programs
Govern Every synthetic example is traceable to its generation parameters and constraints
Control Knobs

Runtime behavior levers

Named, auditable parameters that tune model behavior without retraining

Speed Adjust model behavior in hours, not retraining cycles no ML team required
Govern Explicit, named parameters auditors see what was changed, when, and by whom
Speed Business teams can tune outputs for new domains without engineering bottlenecks
Govern Change history and rollback every parameter state is a versioned snapshot

The Architectural Insight

Why the standoff ends with Knobs

Most enterprises try to reconcile speed and governance at the process level review boards, approval gates, compliance reviews. Knobs make it a structural property of the system itself.

🎯

One artifact, two readers

A Knob is the unit both teams work with. Speed teams compose Knobs to build fast. Governance teams inspect Knobs to verify behavior. There is no translation layer, no handoff document, no meeting between them.

⚡

Speed through precision, not volume

GenAI teams move faster with Knobs because they're targeting high-information regions not drowning in data, not prompting blindly. Precision is the velocity advantage, not raw generation speed.

🛡

Governance without a separate system

Control Knobs are named, versioned parameters not opaque prompt hacks. When auditors ask "what changed and why did the output shift?" the answer is a Knob name, a timestamp, and a value diff.

🔁

The closed loop eliminates drift

Because monitoring feeds back into Discover, the system catches behavioral drift before it compounds. Speed teams get early signal. Governance teams get automated tripwires. Neither team has to build this it's in the loop.

📐

Information geometry as the guardrail

Creation Knobs don't generate arbitrary synthetic data they're bounded by the information geometry of the operational state space. This is what prevents synthetic data from introducing distribution shift that governance teams fear.

🏢

CIO-defensible by design

Every Knob carries metadata: what it controls, its acceptable operating range, its history. When the board asks "can you explain why the model made that decision?" EKIP has a structured, traceable answer ready.
