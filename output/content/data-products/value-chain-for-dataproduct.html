Human + Machine

The AI-Driven
Value Chain.

Data is inert; AI is dynamic. A genuinely smart data product generates cues that stimulate human thinking, ultimately influencing user actions to achieve tangible business results.

Explore the Triad

From Machine Logic to Human Action

A data product powered by AI facilitates the connection between intricate algorithms and tangible results by guiding a targeted three-step psychological shift.

1. Intelligent Signal

The machine learning engine produces synthesized calculations, such as anomalies, forecasts, or probabilistic recommendations, rather than raw data.

The Machine Says: "Probability of User_ID_84 churning in 7 days is 89%."

2. Cognitive Output

When users are able to comprehend data products, they can gain clarity, insight, and understanding through the UX design translating signals into context.

The Human Understands: Oh no, our biggest corporate client is dissatisfied and on the verge of terminating their agreement.

3. Behavioral Output

The user's behavior is optimized in the real world due to the clarity provided by cognitive output.

The Human Acts: Account manager immediately calls the client with a retention offer.
The End Goal

Insight → Decision → Action → Impact

If a data product only provides insights, it is considered a failure. The AI architecture's main goal is to guide the user towards making a significant impact.

1. Insight

The AI model provides clarity. The user understands what is happening and why.

2. Decision

With insight at their disposal, the user assesses their choices and selects the best path ahead.

3. Action

The user's decision is carried out through their workflow or application, resulting in a behavioral output.

4. Impact

The ultimate outcome for a business includes saving revenue, optimizing margins, and mitigating risk.

Design for Behavioral Change

If your data product isn't influencing behavior, it's not creating value. Discover how to create UX that translates AI insights into tangible results.
