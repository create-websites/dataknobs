The problem

AI systems are powerful, but too often opaque.

  • Teams cannot see which variables actually drive AI outcomes.
  • LLM and agent evaluations become expensive and fragmented.
  • Autonomous agents need real-time boundaries, telemetry, and override paths.
  • Governance teams need evidence, not vague confidence.

The Knobs answer

Find the knobs. Measure them. Control them.

  • Discover the high-impact behavioral, data, model, and workflow variables.
  • Turn those variables into evaluation dimensions and operating controls.
  • Continuously optimize AI products for safety, cost, quality, and business outcomes.
  • Give product, engineering, risk, and business teams one shared control plane.
